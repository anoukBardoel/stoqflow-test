# StokPad — Deliveries

StokPad is our lightweight warehouse management system. Day to day, goods also arrive at the warehouse without a purchase order behind them, and right now those receipts can only be recorded one product at a time through manual stock adjustments. We need a new **Deliveries** feature.

## Requirements

- A view where a user records an incoming delivery as multiple rows, one row per product/location combination
- The product and location fields offer suggestions
- After booking in, an on-screen report shows each delivered product, where it went, and the stock before/after
