# StokPad — Deliveries

StokPad is our lightweight warehouse management system. Day to day, goods also arrive at the warehouse in large deliveries, and right now those receipts can only be recorded one product at a time through manual stock adjustments. We need a new **Delivery** feature.

## Requirements

- A view where a user records an incoming delivery as multiple rows, one row per product/location combination
- The product and location fields offer suggestions
- After booking in, an on-screen report shows each delivered product, where it went, and the stock before/after
- One small extra feature of your own choosing that makes the delivery UI genuinely more useful. Keep it small.
